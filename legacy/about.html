About BiteX

We connect you with the best local kitchens and get fresh food to your door, fast. BiteX started as a small idea between friends who were tired of choosing between good food and quick delivery — so we built a platform that does both. BiteX 3.0 is our biggest redesign yet: faster, warmer, and built around the food itself.

🚀 Fast Delivery

Average delivery time under 30 minutes across every partner restaurant.

🍽️ Quality First

We partner only with kitchens that meet our hygiene and quality standards.

💚 Fair to Partners

Restaurants and riders on BiteX earn fair, transparent commissions.

📍 Always Nearby

Now live in over 40 cities, with new neighbourhoods added every month.
